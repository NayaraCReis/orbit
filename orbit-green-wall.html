The Currency
EcoCredits — earned, not bought

EcoCredits are soulbound tokens — they cannot be purchased, transferred, or speculated. They are earned through verified actions: returning containers, qualifying purchases (local, organic, plant-based, package-free), and completing your EcoQuiz journey. This is what makes them trustworthy as a currency of real behaviour change.

🥬
The Exchange
Food as the unit of real value

Why food? Because everyone needs it. Because it connects to the most impactful daily choices — what you eat, where it comes from, how it's packaged. FoodPacks from partner retailers are tokenised with contents, location, and carbon score. EcoCredits redeem them. No food is wasted. No value is lost. The loop closes.

🏘️
The Redistribution
Wealth flows to those who generate it

Ireland's €66.7M in unclaimed deposits is a public good fund that doesn't yet know it exists. ORBIT unlocks it democratically. Users choose: receive value as food credits or donate to vulnerable households, school programmes, and community food initiatives. The act of recycling becomes an act of solidarity.

How ORBIT makes money
Four honest revenue streams — none of them extractive, all of them circular.
01
Brand Partnership Fees

Sustainable brands pay to feature in the Green Wall and EcoQuiz results. Ethical, context-first placement — your credit only converts at brands that earned their score. No extractive advertising. Algorithmic ranking by real sustainability metrics.

€500–€2,000 / month per brand
02
FoodPack Margin

Partner retailers list surplus food as FoodPacks. ORBIT takes a small transaction margin on each redemption — while the retailer reduces waste and gains a new customer channel. Everyone wins. The food that would have been binned feeds someone.

5–8% margin per FoodPack
03
Impact Data Reports

Aggregated, anonymised behavioural data sold as sustainability reports to Government, Re-turn Ireland, and corporate ESG teams. Shows the collective impact of circular participation. Never personal data. Always community intelligence.

€5,000–€20,000 per report / licence
04
Community Fund Grants

ORBIT is built for grant funding: EPA Ireland, Sustainable Energy Authority, Enterprise Ireland, EU Green Deal, Community Foundation Ireland. The redistribution mission and measurable behaviour change metrics make it a strong fit for public funding in year one.

€50k–€500k target range year 1

The full loop
explained simply

1
Physical Layer
You return a container at a Re-turn kiosk

Any plastic bottle, can, or carton covered by Ireland's Deposit Return Scheme. The kiosk scans it, validates it, and sends the deposit signal to ORBIT's API. This is where money enters the system.

⊙ 1 EcoCredit minted per container
2
Onboarding Choice
You choose your participation mode

At first sign-up: do you want to receive value as food credits for yourself, or donate your credits to vulnerable households and community food programmes? You can change this anytime. Both choices are honoured equally — no moral hierarchy.

3
EcoQuiz
You take the EcoQuiz — 9 dimensions, 18 questions

Your footprint across Mind & Body, Work, Fashion, Home, Travel, Community, Buying, Eating, and Civic engagement. Score out of 54. Your Resonance Level determines your starting tier and which behaviour journey unlocks first.

⊙ Quiz score = your EcoWallet tier
4
Behaviour Engine
You make qualifying purchases — and credits multiply

Shopping local, organic, plant-based, or package-free at a verified Green Algorithm partner triggers a ×2 or ×3 credit multiplier. The triple signal (quiz tier + purchase + location) validates it. Gaming is structurally prevented.

⊙ Up to ×3 EcoCredit multiplier
5
Redistribution
Credits become food — or become someone else's food

FoodPacks from partner retailers (surplus food tokenised with contents, location, and carbon score) are redeemed with EcoCredits. If you've chosen donate mode, your value flows directly to vulnerable households via community hubs — no app required on the receiving end.

⊙ The loop closes here
6
Policy Layer
Your behaviour becomes a democratic argument

Aggregated, anonymised data from thousands of users builds the evidence base: unclaimed deposits should be a public good fund. ORBIT presents this to Re-turn Ireland, the EPA, and the Government — turning a community platform into a policy lever.

⊙ Individual action → systemic change
